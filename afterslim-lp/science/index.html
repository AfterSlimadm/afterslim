The research behind AfterSlim

GLP-1 medications work because they slow the gut. That same slowdown shifts the microbiome and depletes the strains responsible for digestive comfort and energy. AfterSlim is built to replenish what GLP-1s deplete.

How GLP-1 medications affect your gut

A four-step chain that turns a weight-loss benefit into the side effects most people don't see coming.

1

Slowed gastric emptying

GLP-1s like Ozempic and Mounjaro extend the time food sits in the stomach and small intestine.

2

Microbiome shift

The longer transit time changes which strains thrive. Beneficial species lose ground.

3

Akkermansia declines

The keystone strain that maintains the mucus layer drops, weakening the gut barrier.

4

Symptoms surface

Nausea, constipation, bloating, fatigue, and the brain fog people report on GLP-1s.

What's in the capsule

Three keystone probiotic strains paired with two prebiotic fibers chosen specifically to feed them. Every dose matches what clinical research uses.

3 Keystone Strains

Mucus-layer keystone

Akkermansia muciniphila

Lives in the gut's mucus layer and helps maintain the barrier between gut contents and bloodstream. The strain GLP-1 medications most consistently deplete.

Depommier C, et al. Supplementation with Akkermansia muciniphila in overweight and obese human volunteers. Nature Medicine, 2019.

Butyrate producer

Clostridium butyricum

Ferments prebiotic fiber into butyrate, the short-chain fatty acid that fuels colon cells and supports the integrity of the gut barrier.

Stoeva MK, et al. Butyrate-producing human gut symbiont Clostridium butyricum and its role in health and disease. Gut Microbes, 2021.

GI comfort strain

Bifidobacterium infantis

One of the most clinically studied probiotics for digestive comfort. Reduces gas production and supports regularity, especially during slow-transit periods.

Whorwell PJ, et al. Efficacy of an encapsulated probiotic Bifidobacterium infantis 35624 in women with irritable bowel syndrome. American Journal of Gastroenterology, 2006.

2 Prebiotics That Feed Them

Akkermansia fuel

Chicory Root Inulin

A soluble fiber that selectively feeds Akkermansia muciniphila. Without dedicated fuel, the strain you add doesn't establish.

Cani PD, et al. Selective increases of bifidobacteria in gut microflora improve high-fat-diet-induced diabetes in mice. Diabetologia, 2007. Replicated in human trials showing ~3x Akkermansia abundance over 8 weeks.

Colonic fermentation

Resistant Starch

Reaches the colon undigested where it ferments into butyrate. Also linked to improved insulin sensitivity, complementing the GLP-1 mechanism without overlapping it.

Robertson MD, et al. Insulin-sensitizing effects of dietary resistant starch and effects on skeletal muscle and adipose tissue metabolism. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2005.
