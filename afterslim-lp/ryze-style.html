Nausea & Bloating

Why this happens: GLP-1 puts the brakes on your stomach. Food sits there for hours longer than it should.

Your stomach was designed to empty in about 90 minutes. On Ozempic, Mounjaro, or Wegovy, that stretches past 4 hours. The prolonged fullness triggers the nausea. The food fermenting while it waits creates the gas and bloating.

Constipation & Cramps

Why this happens: GLP-1 doesn't just slow your stomach. It slows your entire digestive tract.

Slower transit means harder, drier stool. And when you're eating less, you're also taking in less fiber and less water. That's the perfect storm for the cramps and the "Ozempic stuck" feeling users describe in forums.

Why this happens: Less food plus altered gut bacteria means a smaller battery to run on.

Your gut microbes don't just digest food. They make short-chain fatty acids, small molecules that feed your colon cells and your mitochondria. GLP-1 medications disrupt that ecosystem, and with less food coming in, your body runs on a smaller battery.
