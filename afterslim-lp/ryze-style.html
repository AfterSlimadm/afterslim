01 / Gut

Nausea & Bloating

Why this happens: GLP-1 slows gastric emptying — food sits in your stomach for hours longer than normal.

Your stomach was designed to empty in about 90 minutes. On Ozempic, Mounjaro, or Wegovy, that can stretch past 4 hours. The food fermenting in there is what creates the fullness that won't quit, the queasiness, and the gas.

~44% of users on semaglutide report nausea in clinical trials.

02 / Gut

Constipation & Cramps

Why this happens: GLP-1 doesn't just slow your stomach — it slows your entire digestive tract.

Slower transit means harder, drier stool. Combined with eating less, you're also taking in less fiber and less water — the perfect storm for the cramps and the "Ozempic stuck" feeling so many users describe in forums.

20–30% of GLP-1 users experience constipation severe enough to report it.

03 / Visible

Hair Loss & Brittle Nails

Why this happens: Rapid caloric restriction triggers telogen effluvium — your body pauses hair growth to conserve protein.

When you suddenly eat 30–40% less, your body deprioritizes "optional" systems first. Hair follicles and nail beds don't get the amino acids and biotin they need. The shedding usually shows up 2–3 months after starting the medication — exactly when you're celebrating the weight loss.

Reported by 3–7% in trials. Real-world reports run 3–4× higher.

04 / Energy

Fatigue & Low Energy

Why this happens: Reduced food intake plus altered gut bacteria equals reduced cellular energy.

Your gut microbes don't just digest food — they produce short-chain fatty acids that fuel your colon cells and influence your mitochondria. GLP-1 medications disrupt this ecosystem, and combined with eating less, your body runs on a smaller battery.

~1 in 7 users report persistent fatigue interfering with daily life.

05 / Metabolic

Sugar Cravings & Hunger Spikes

Why this happens: When the medication wears off late in the cycle, your unbalanced gut screams for what's missing.

GLP-1 medications mute hunger but don't fix the microbiome — the bacteria that quietly regulate satiety, blood sugar, and the reward signals around food. When the drug ebbs, your brain hears the unbalanced gut and reaches for the fastest fuel it knows: sugar.

Most users report rebound cravings between doses — the #1 reason people fall off the regimen.

06 / Body

Muscle Loss

Why this happens: Up to 40% of the weight you lose on GLP-1 is lean mass — not fat.

Without enough protein, resistance training, and recovery support, your body burns muscle alongside fat. That's why GLP-1 users often look thinner but feel weaker — and gain the weight back faster the moment they stop the medication. The hidden cost no one talks about.

25–40% of weight lost on GLP-1 is lean mass (recent meta-analyses).
