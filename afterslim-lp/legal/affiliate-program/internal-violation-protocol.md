# AfterSlim Affiliate Program: Internal Violation Protocol

> **NOTICE:** This document is not legal advice and is intended as a starting framework subject to legal review. ---

## Purpose

This protocol defines how the AfterSlim team handles affiliate violations of the [Affiliate Agreement](./affiliate-agreement.md) and the [Content Guidelines](./content-guidelines.md). The goals, in order:

1. **Protect the brand** from FTC, FDA, platform, and reputational risk.
2. **Treat affiliates fairly** with consistent escalation and a documented right of appeal.
3. **Show good faith** if an external party (FTC, FDA, plaintiff's lawyer, journalist) ever investigates the Program.

Consistency is what makes this defensible. Inconsistent enforcement undermines the program legally and culturally. **Follow the protocol even when it feels heavy-handed**, and document every action.

## Severity Levels

### Level 1: Warning

**Triggers (illustrative, not exhaustive):**

- Missing or incomplete FTC disclosure (forgot `#ad`, buried in hashtag block).
- Disclosure placement issue (only in description, only in bio).
- Missing FDA disclaimer on a structure/function post.
- Borderline claim that approaches but does not cross a prohibited claim (e.g., "transformed my gut" without quantified weight loss).
- Use of slightly off-brand logo or color but no material misrepresentation.
- First-time minor brand asset misuse.

**Response:**

1. **Within 24 hours of detection:** Vitor screenshots the offending content and logs it in the [Compliance Log](./compliance-log-template.md).
2. **Within 48 hours of detection:** Vitor sends the affiliate the Level 1 Warning email (template below).
3. The email requires the affiliate to:
   - Correct or remove the content within **48 hours** of receipt.
   - Confirm completion by replying to the email.
4. Vitor adds the incident to the affiliate's record in the Affiliate Dashboard. A counter is incremented.

**Threshold:** **Three** Level 1 warnings within a rolling 90-day period escalate the affiliate automatically to Level 2 on the next incident.

**No payout impact** at Level 1, unless the affiliate fails to correct within the 48-hour window. Failure to correct converts the incident to Level 2.

---

### Level 2: Suspension

**Triggers (illustrative):**

- Repeat Level 1 violation within 90 days.
- Failure to correct a Level 1 violation within the 48-hour window.
- Mild but unmistakable disease claim ("helps treat IBS", "for diabetes patients").
- Use of GLP-1 drug names as primary keywords in content.
- Posting a before-and-after framed as caused by the Product.
- Failure to use the FDA disclaimer on a viral post (5,000+ views, plays, or impressions).
- Running paid ads using brand assets without pre-approval.

**Response:**

1. **Within 24 hours of detection:** Document with screenshots, URLs, timestamps. Save to the Compliance Log.
2. **Within 24 hours of documentation:** Vitor brings to Allan or Henrique for signoff.
3. **Upon signoff:** Affiliate's status is set to **Revoked** in the Affiliate Dashboard, **pending payouts are placed on hold**, and the Level 2 Suspension email is sent.
4. Affiliate has **7 calendar days** to:
   - Remove the violating content.
   - Reply confirming removal with screenshots of corrected state.
   - Acknowledge the violation in writing.
5. If the affiliate completes these steps to AfterSlim's satisfaction, status is restored to **Active**, held payouts are released, and the incident is closed at Level 2.
6. If the affiliate does not comply within 7 days, the incident automatically escalates to Level 3.

**Tracking:** **Two** Level 2 incidents within a 12-month period escalate the affiliate automatically to Level 3 on the next incident.

---

### Level 3: Termination

**Triggers (illustrative):**

- Aggressive disease, cure, or treatment claims ("cures diabetes", "ends weight gain forever", "FDA approved").
- Targeting minors or vulnerable populations.
- Repeated Level 2 incidents (per the rolling threshold above).
- Failure to comply with a Level 2 corrective action within 7 days.
- Fake reviews on Amazon, the Site, Trustpilot, Google, or any platform.
- Use of fake testimonials, AI-generated likenesses presented as real customers, or fabricated before-and-after photos.
- Cookie stuffing, click fraud, or any attempt to inflate attribution artificially.
- Self-referral abuse (using own link for own purchases, multi-account schemes).
- Selling, transferring, or sharing the affiliate account without authorization.
- Material misrepresentation in the application or W-9.

**Response:**

1. **Within 24 hours of detection:** Document fully with screenshots, URLs, archived web copies (`web.archive.org` or screenshot bundle), timestamps, and any related communications.
2. **Within 48 hours:** Allan and Henrique jointly review the documentation.
3. **Upon decision:** Affiliate status is set to **Banned** in the Affiliate Dashboard. All unpaid Commissions are **forfeited and clawed back** under Section 14 of the Affiliate Agreement. The Level 3 Termination email is sent.
4. Tracking pixels and slug are deactivated within 24 hours.
5. The affiliate is added to an internal "do not approve" list keyed on email, name, and tax ID. Future applications from the same identity are auto-rejected.
6. If the violating content is on the affiliate's owned property (their site, their account), the email demands removal within 7 days and threatens legal action if not removed.
7. If the violating content is on a third-party platform (Amazon reviews, Google reviews), AfterSlim reports the content directly to the platform.

**Appeal rights:** The affiliate has **7 calendar days** from the termination notice to file a written appeal under Section 12 of the [Content Guidelines](./content-guidelines.md). Appeal is reviewed by the founder who did **not** issue the termination decision. Appeal outcomes are final.

---

### Level 4: Legal Escalation

**Triggers (illustrative):**

- FTC, FDA, state attorney general, or state DOJ inquiry naming AfterSlim or referencing affiliate conduct.
- Cease-and-desist letter received from any party related to affiliate conduct.
- Civil litigation filed or threatened against AfterSlim arising from affiliate conduct.
- Platform-level enforcement action (e.g., Meta or TikTok suspending AfterSlim's brand account based on affiliate behavior).
- Significant reputational damage event (viral negative coverage, journalist inquiry, mass complaints).
- Affiliate engaging in conduct that may constitute a crime (fraud, identity theft, stalking, harassment).

**Response:**

1. **Within 4 hours of detection:** Allan is notified directly by phone or signal channel. Henrique is informed.
2. **Within 24 hours:** Allan retains, or escalates to, Florida counsel for the matter. Internal team **does not respond to the inquiring party directly** without counsel's review and approval.
3. The affected affiliate (if known) is moved immediately to **Banned** status. All payouts are frozen. All tracking is disabled.
4. A litigation hold is initiated: do not delete any emails, Slack/internal messages, screenshots, dashboards, or system logs related to the affiliate or the incident. Notify the team in writing.
5. All external communications, including platform reports, social posts, and PR statements, route through counsel.
6. Document every communication in a dedicated incident folder.

**Public statements:** Make none without counsel approval. "We are reviewing the matter and take all such concerns seriously" is a safe placeholder; anything beyond that needs review.

## Documentation Requirements

For every incident at any level, the following must be captured and saved to the Compliance Log:

- Date and time of detection (ISO 8601, with timezone).
- Affiliate identifier (slug, email, name).
- Platform and URL where the violating content was found.
- Screenshot of the content. PDF print is acceptable if a screenshot is not feasible.
- Archived web copy (use `archive.org/save/` for public URLs) for content that may be edited or deleted.
- The specific Agreement or Guidelines section violated.
- Severity level assigned.
- Action taken and timestamp.
- Affiliate response (or non-response) and timestamp.
- Final resolution and timestamp.
- Decision-maker(s) and reviewer (for Level 2+).

Documentation is the difference between a defensible program and an indefensible one. If an FTC investigator ever asks "how do you enforce this?" the answer should be the Compliance Log.

## Appeal Process

When an affiliate appeals a Level 2 or Level 3 decision:

1. The appeal must be received by **support@afterslim.com** within **7 calendar days** of the original notice.
2. The appeal must include: the affiliate's slug, the decision being appealed, the basis for the appeal, and any supporting evidence.
3. The appeal is logged in the Compliance Log immediately on receipt.
4. The appeal is reviewed by a founder who did **not** issue the underlying decision. If the original decision was joint, an outside reviewer (e.g., an advisor or counsel) may be designated.
5. The review is completed within **10 business days**.
6. The outcome is communicated to the affiliate in writing and logged.
7. The appeal outcome is **final**. No second-level appeal exists within the Program.
