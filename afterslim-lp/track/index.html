How tracking works

You'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. That link takes you straight to the carrier (USPS, UPS, or FedEx) where you can see live updates.

I haven't received a tracking email

  • Order placed in the last 24 hours? Your tracking email arrives once we hand the package to the carrier (usually 1 to 2 business days after you order).
  • Order placed more than 2 business days ago? Check your spam/junk folder and search for "AfterSlim" or "shipping."
  • Still nothing? Email us at support@afterslim.com with your order number and we'll resend it.

The tracking link hasn't updated

Carriers sometimes scan a package then take 24 to 48 hours to update tracking. If your package hasn't moved in more than 3 business days, contact us and we'll investigate.

The package shows delivered but I didn't get it

  • Check with neighbors and around the property (porch, garage, behind plants)
  • Wait 24 hours — carriers occasionally scan as "delivered" a day early
  • Contact us at support@afterslim.com if it still hasn't shown up. We'll send a replacement.
