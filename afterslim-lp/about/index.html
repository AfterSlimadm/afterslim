Why we exist

Tens of millions of people are now on GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, and Zepbound. They are seeing real results. They are also dealing with side effects almost nobody talks about: nausea that lasts for weeks, constipation that turns dinner out into a panic, bloating that makes the weight loss invisible in the mirror.

Most of that comes down to the gut. GLP-1s slow digestion, shift the microbiome, and deplete one specific strain (Akkermansia muciniphila) that the gut depends on. Existing probiotics weren't built for that. They were built for general digestive health, with strains chosen because they're cheap to manufacture.

AfterSlim is one capsule a day, formulated end-to-end for one situation: keeping your gut working while a GLP-1 medication does its job.
