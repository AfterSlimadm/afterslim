Nausea & Bloating

Why this happens: GLP-1 puts the brakes on your stomach. Food sits there for hours longer than it should.

Your stomach was designed to empty in about 90 minutes. On Ozempic, Mounjaro, or Wegovy, that stretches past 4 hours. The prolonged fullness triggers the nausea. The food fermenting while it waits creates the gas and bloating.

How common this is

44%

Nearly 1 in 2 GLP-1 users report nausea in clinical trials.

On GLP-1 240+min

Constipation & Cramps

Why this happens: GLP-1 doesn't just slow your stomach. It slows your entire digestive tract.

Slower transit means harder, drier stool. And when you're eating less, you're also taking in less fiber and less water. That's the perfect storm for the cramps and the "Ozempic stuck" feeling users describe in forums.

How common this is

30%

Up to 1 in 3 GLP-1 users report constipation severe enough to call out.

How long food takes to pass

Normal 30hrs
On GLP-1 60+hrs

Hair Loss & Brittle Nails

Why this happens: Eating much less puts hair growth on pause. Your body keeps the protein for vital organs first.

When you suddenly eat 30–40% less, your body pauses "optional" systems first. Hair follicles and nail beds stop getting the amino acids and biotin they need. The shedding usually shows up 2–3 months later, right when you're celebrating the weight loss.

How common this is

20%

Trials say 3–7%. Real-world reports run 3–4× higher, closer to 1 in 5.

Fatigue & Low Energy

Why this happens: Less food plus altered gut bacteria means a smaller battery to run on.

Your gut microbes don't just digest food. They make short-chain fatty acids, small molecules that feed your colon cells and your mitochondria. GLP-1 medications disrupt that ecosystem, and with less food coming in, your body runs on a smaller battery.

How common this is

14%

About 1 in 7 GLP-1 users report fatigue serious enough to interfere with daily life.

Sugar Cravings & Hunger Spikes

Why this happens: When the medication fades late in the cycle, your unbalanced gut screams for what's missing.

GLP-1 medications mute hunger but don't fix the microbiome. Those bacteria quietly regulate satiety, blood sugar, and the reward signals around food. When the drug ebbs, your brain hears the unbalanced gut and reaches for the fastest fuel it knows: sugar.

How common this is

70%

About 7 in 10 GLP-1 users describe rebound cravings between doses. It's the #1 reason people drop off the regimen.

Why this happens: Up to 40% of the weight you lose on GLP-1 isn't fat. It's muscle.

Without enough protein, resistance training, and recovery support, your body burns muscle alongside fat. That's why GLP-1 users often look thinner but feel weaker. And it's why the weight comes back faster the moment they stop the medication. The part of weight loss that gets less attention.
