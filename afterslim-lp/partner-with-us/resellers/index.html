Why we do it this way

AfterSlim works because of repetition. People feel the difference around week 2, the deeper benefits land at month 2 or 3. The customers who refer us are usually the ones who already see that, and we want to make it worth their time without turning them into salespeople.

Store credit makes that simple. You share a link, your friend orders, the credit lands in your account and the next bottle costs less. No new payment system to learn, no W-9, no taxes to file.

Quick questions

Do I have to apply?

No. Every AfterSlim customer is automatically a Reseller. Your link is waiting on the dashboard the moment your first order ships.

What does "store credit" actually mean?

Credit is dollars you can spend at AfterSlim. It applies to your next order automatically at checkout. It does not convert to cash, does not expire, and stacks with the 5% cashback you already earn on your own purchases.

What if my friend cancels or refunds?

The credit is reversed. We only pay on orders that actually clear the 90-day satisfaction window.

Can I refer myself with a second account?

No. We block self-referrals using email, payment method, and IP signal. Use it for friends and family who would actually benefit.

What about influencers with a large audience?

The reseller path is for personal referrals, capped at $100 of credit per month. If you have an audience to reach, look at the Ambassador program instead, it pays cash and is uncapped.
