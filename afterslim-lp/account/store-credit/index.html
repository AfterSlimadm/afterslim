Redeem your balance as a coupon?

Your $0.00 balance becomes a single-use coupon. You can't get it back as balance later. The coupon is valid for 7 days; if you don't use it in time, the credit is forfeited.

How you earn

5% of the product subtotal of every completed order returns to your balance, including subscription shipments.

How it applies

Sign in before checking out. Your available balance shows up as a one-click discount, applied up to the order total. No code needed.

What if I refund?

Refunded orders forfeit the credit earned from that order. If you've already spent it, the refund will return what's left after that deduction.
